UI/UX Works:

Segundo

As the UI/UX industry shifted toward Figma, I took the opportunity to expand my skill set by learning the platform while developing a complete UI/UX project. Inspired by Segundo Coffee Lab, a Houston-based coffee shop where I previously worked, I developed a mobile ordering app concept that reflects the brand’s identity and cultural roots. Drawing from its existing visual language, I incorporated warm orange accents, muted gray tones, and a clean, intuitive interface. Through this hands-on learning experience, I worked through the design process from initial research and wireframes to a high-fidelity, interactive prototype, focusing on a streamlined customer ordering experience.
