CPL-Air

CPL-Air was my first paid client project, created for the owner of an HVAC company who came to me with an initial concept for his logo. Working within a tight schedule, I developed the idea in Illustrator, refining the color palette, typography, and shield design through client feedback. The final identity uses color to represent heating and cooling without relying on typical HVAC imagery and was ultimately implemented on the company’s website.
