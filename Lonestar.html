LoneStar

Throughout my portfolio, I’ve focused on wearing both hats—design and development. After interviewing for a WordPress-focused position, however, I saw an opportunity to specialize and strengthen my experience with a workflow commonly used by agencies and studios. I created a simulated client handoff for LoneStar Field Solutions, using an established design and assets so I could focus specifically on building the site with WordPress and Elementor. From there, I went beyond the page builder to explore responsive implementation, child themes, custom CSS and PHP, and SEO fundamentals, giving me a better understanding of how a professional WordPress project comes together from handoff to finished site.
